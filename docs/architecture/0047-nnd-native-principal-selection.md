# NND native principal selection under unresolved activation

Status: private prerequisite. It does not activate NND or change request
permissions.

After exact post-publication health and the dark controller challenge, the
held-owner callback may consume its short-lived, one-use same-process proof
into the existing native service. The selection binds the proof to that exact
native listener, trial state, selected operation and generation, and the
original service and package-registry owners. A replay or a changed listener
fails. The native service retains only unresolved evidence; reading it again
rechecks that the closed-only trial admission gate and listener remain live.

The listener continues to resolve the limited trial principal. Switching it
to the ordinary operator principal now would broaden GET access while the
closed-only gate only rejects non-GET/HEAD methods. In particular, native
secret and provider inventory reads would become available before the
activation barrier is retired. The selection therefore has no method to open
the gate or promote effective authority. The current trial runner still
stops the child after the callback, so this state is ephemeral and cannot be
treated as a durable `completed` decision.

A later live-owner transfer must consume the selected evidence while the
same child, listener, controller, leases, registration and pointer are still
verified. It must also prove a private ticket/attach path, settle a durable
completion and barrier-retirement protocol, and only then make ordinary
operator authority and public attach effective. Crash recovery cannot infer
that authority survived from this in-memory selection.
